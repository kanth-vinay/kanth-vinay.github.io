    1. Mean vs. Median

    The Problem

    Here are 7 exam scores:

    68, 71, 74, 75, 77, 79, 81

    The mean and median are roughly 75. Suppose the last score was entered with a typo: 10 instead of 81.

    One outlier can secretly drag the average (mean) far from where most of the data actually sits.

    R is Powerful, Not Complicated

    You do not need to be a computer scientist. R is simply a text-based calculator. We provide the code.

    The Old Way (Spreadsheets)

    • Requires highlighting thousands of cells.
    • Formulas are hidden inside cells and break easily.
    • Crashes when analyzing large industry datasets.

    Our Way (RStudio)

    Type one command. Process data instantly.

    # Find the average score
    mean(student_scores)

    # Find the standard deviation
    sd(student_scores)

    You run it. You interpret it. That is it.

    Why R? It is the industry standard analytical tool in Healthcare, Finance, Tech, and Sports. Learning it makes you highly employable.
