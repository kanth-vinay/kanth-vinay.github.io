II · Research with a student, ICERI 2026

Recognition without reasoning

Version A

Which is a root of x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0?

(a) 1   (b) 2   (c) 4   (d) 5

Version B

Find the roots. Sketch y = x^2 - 5x + 6.
Why does it dip below the axis?

Given choices, students eliminate. They stop reasoning.

About 4 minutes. A weak student substitutes each option. Version B needs algebra, picture and meaning together.
III · How, with chalk only

Four moves for tomorrow's class

1. Predict, then reveal

Before solving, every student writes a guess. The gap between guess and answer is where learning starts.

2. The planted error

Solve on the board with one deliberate mistake. The class finds it and explains why it is wrong.

3. One rich problem

One problem, three methods, compared, instead of twenty exercises of the same kind.

4. Explain it in the notebook

One line of "why" under every answer. The notebook becomes the record of reasoning.

About 6 minutes. Each move costs nothing and works with forty students. Give one example of each from your own teaching.
