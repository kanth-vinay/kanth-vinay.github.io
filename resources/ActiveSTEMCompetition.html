Q1 GRADES 3–5

Rectangular Garden Setup

A rectangular garden measures 18 m long and 10 m wide.

A straight path of uniform width 2 m runs along the entire length of the garden on one side.

Inside the remaining garden area, a square herb plot of side length 4 m is placed in one corner.

What is the exact area of the remaining garden — the part that is neither path nor herb plot?

Q2 GRADES 3–5

The Morning Allocation

Every morning before the gates open at the St. Louis Summer Fair, a juice vendor prepares exactly 48 litres of freshly squeezed tropical juice. The fair runs six days a week throughout the summer season.

Before serving begins, the vendor sets aside one third of the total juice for the breakfast session. From what remains after the breakfast portion has been allocated, one quarter is held back for the lunch crowd. Everything left after both the breakfast and lunch portions have been set aside is served during the dinner hour.

On a typical morning, how many litres of juice are available at the dinner session?

Q3 GRADES 3–5

The Weighing Protocol

At a robot testing facility, three robots — Sparky, Clank, and Bolt — are weighed in pairs as part of a routine calibration check before the summer competition. The facility's scale can only weigh two robots at a time. The testing engineer records the following three readings:

Sparky + Clank
30 lbs
Clank + Bolt
40 lbs
Sparky + Bolt
50 lbs

The facility's inventory system requires the individual weight of each robot to be logged separately. All three weights must be whole numbers of pounds.

Determine the exact individual weight of each robot. State all three weights clearly and show every step of your reasoning.

Q5 GRADES 3–5

The Library Schedule

The Daniel Boone branch of the St. Louis County Library is hosting a Saturday morning of back-to-back activities for summer learners. A student arrives for the first workshop at 10:15 AM and stays through all three workshops, including the breaks between them. During the second break, the student steps outside to make a quick phone call to a cousin in India, to describe what was learned in Workshop 2.

SATURDAY WORKSHOP SCHEDULE
10:15 AMWorkshop 1 begins
50 minWorkshop 1 duration
15 minBreak 1
1 hr 10 minWorkshop 2 duration
20 minBreak 2 (call to India)
35 minWorkshop 3 duration
PART A
At what time does Workshop 3 end? Show your time calculations step by step.
PART B
How much total time did the student spend inside the three workshops (excluding breaks)? Give your answer in hours and minutes.
PART C
India is 10 hr 30 min ahead of St. Louis. At what time (Indian time) does the cousin receive the call during Break 2?

Q2 GRADES 6–8

Error Analysis

The expression to simplify is:

$$E = 5[3x - 2(x - 4)] - \frac{1}{2}(6x - 8) + 3(2x + 1) - (4x - 7)$$

A student has submitted the simplification below. It contains three algebraic errors.

Given $E = 5[3x - 2(x - 4)] - \frac{1}{2}(6x - 8) + 3(2x + 1) - (4x - 7)$
[Line 1] $= 5[3x - 2x - 4] - \frac{1}{2}(6x - 8) + 3(2x + 1) - (4x - 7)$
[Line 2] $= 5(x - 4) - \frac{1}{2}(6x - 8) + 3(2x + 1) - (4x - 7)$
[Line 3] $= 5x - 20 - (3x - 8) + (6x + 3) - (4x - 7)$
[Line 4] $= 5x - 20 - 3x + 8 + 6x + 3 - 4x - 7$
[Line 5] $= (5x - 3x + 6x - 4x) + (-20 + 8 + 3 - 7)$
[Line 6] $= 4x - 16$

YOUR TASK

  • (a) Study every line carefully. Identify each line containing an error.
  • (b) For each error, state what the algebraic mistake is and what the correct result should be.
  • (c) Solve the expression correctly from the beginning, showing every step in full.
